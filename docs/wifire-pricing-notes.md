## Quotation rules

1. Confirm site, expected concurrent users, indoor/outdoor zones, power, ISP and
   mounting conditions.
2. Refresh supplier prices and stock.
3. Take a deposit before buying equipment.
4. Treat software/setup/hardware as separate quotation lines.
5. State that AP quantity and coverage are subject to site validation.
6. Do not promise live payment processing until provider onboarding, backend
   hosting and production tests are complete.
7. Tax treatment must reflect BrimstoneTech's actual registration status at the
   date of sale; never invent a VAT-inclusive claim.
