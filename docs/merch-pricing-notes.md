Forge Crew remains internal and is not priced for sale.

## Formula warning

- 75% markup: `selling price = landed cost × 1.75`
- 75% gross margin: `selling price = landed cost ÷ 0.25`

The public estimates use **75% markup**, not a 75% gross margin.
