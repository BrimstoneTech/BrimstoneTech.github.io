Single-business, single-deployment licence

Purchase permits the named client to operate one Wi-Fire deployment and keep reasonable operational backups. Source-code resale, sublicensing, redistribution, product cloning and unauthorized copying are not permitted. Expansion to another independently operated site requires a written licence extension or additional deployment licence.

What “deployment-ready” means: the portal, architecture, contracts and router starter configuration are prepared for implementation. The public prototype does not process live money. A working site still requires an approved Mobile Money merchant account or payment provider, a secure hosted backend, an ISP connection that permits the intended use, physical hardware, site validation and production testing. Hardware bundles do not include Wi-Fire software, the optional setup fee, cabling runs, installation labour, travel, internet subscription, merchant/provider fees, permits or taxes unless a written quotation says otherwise.
