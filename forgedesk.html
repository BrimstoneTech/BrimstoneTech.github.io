EFRIS / URA notice

A document produced here is not a fiscal invoice. It is not issued through EFRIS and carries no fiscal document number, QR code or URA verification code. If you are VAT-registered in Uganda you must still issue the corresponding e-invoice/e-receipt through EFRIS and follow all Uganda Revenue Authority requirements. Use ForgeDesk output as a commercial quotation or a working draft only.

Data privacy

Your documents are stored only in this browser's localStorage, under the key brimstone.forgedesk.v1. Nothing is uploaded, no analytics run on this page, and no request leaves your device except the Google Fonts stylesheet and the logo image. Clearing site data, using a different browser or a private window means the documents are gone. Use Export JSON for real backups. On a shared computer, use Delete all data when you are done.
